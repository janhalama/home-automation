# Seeing the day

What a person needs when they open a day. The way it is built comes after these are agreed.

## Why

A day is hard to trust when the plan redraws hours that already happened, when a change has no reason next to it, and when the plan and the real flows are easy to mix up.

## See the plan

I open a day and see what was decided, along the clock.

I can tell these apart:

- when the tank was meant to heat
- when the car was meant to charge, and from the grid or from the battery
- when the battery was meant to charge from the grid
- when the battery was meant to wait, so the grid runs the house
- when the battery was meant to run the house
- when the battery was meant to sell

Hours that have already started keep the decision that was in force then. A later decision does not rewrite them.

Hours that have not started show the current decision. That part may change.

A battery that is waiting is a decision I can see. It is not an empty gap.

## See why the plan changed

When the decision for the hours still ahead changes, the day shows a line.

The line says when it changed, what changed, and why, in one sentence. The reason is something I can recognise: the price, how full the battery is, how hot the tank is, what the car still needs, the expected sun, or a decision that finished so the next one could start. Under the line I can ask to see the plan as it was at that moment. It opens over the day: the tank, the car, and the battery along the clock. It is not kept open on the page.

The same decision repeated does not add a line.

The newest lines are on the page for that day.

## See where the energy went

On the same day I see what really happened, separate from the plan:

- the car
- the tank
- the battery
- the grid
- the house

I can read them along the same clock as the plan. A decision and the flow that followed it stay two different pictures.

## See when the house acted on its own

When the heater runs in hours the plan said to heat, it belongs with the plan.

When the heater runs in hours the plan did not say to heat, I can tell that apart. The day says the house did it, not the plan. It does not yet name which switch it was.
